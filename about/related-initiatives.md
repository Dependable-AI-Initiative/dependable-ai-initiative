# Related initiatives

We aim to complement, not duplicate, existing work. This page lists related initiatives, what we can learn from them, and how the Dependable AI Initiative differs.

*Last reviewed: October 2026. Corrections and additions are welcome by pull request.*

| Initiative | Focus | What we can learn | Relationship |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Coalition for Secure AI (CoSAI)](https://github.com/cosai-oasis) (OASIS Open Project) | AI security: supply chain, defenders, risk governance, agentic systems | One repo per workstream; open technical participation funded by sponsors; clear onboarding | Reference and map to the CoSAI Risk Map |
| [OWASP GenAI Security Project](https://github.com/genai-security-project) | LLM and GenAI security risks (Top 10, red teaming, AI BOM) | Umbrella GitHub organisation; `good first issue` labels; initiative-based repos | Map Framework risks to the OWASP Top 10 for LLMs |
| [FINOS AI Governance Framework](https://github.com/finos/ai-governance-framework) | AI governance risks and controls for financial services | Public draft refined through PRs to v1.0; weekly open working sessions; mapping to NIST, OWASP and the EU AI Act | Similar method, different audience (we are cross-sector and focused on open technologies) |
| [UK AISI Inspect / inspect_evals](https://github.com/UKGovernmentBEIS/inspect_evals) | Open-source model evaluation framework and community evaluations | Plan-before-build contributions; peer verification; documented lessons ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.06893)) | Potential foundation for Toolkit reference tests |
| [AI Verify Foundation / Project Moonshot](https://github.com/aiverify-foundation) (Singapore) | Open-source testing toolkit for generative AI | Foundation-governed open toolkit in the Asia-Pacific region | Potential foundation for Toolkit reference tests |

## Where we fit

Existing initiatives largely cover **general AI security**, **sector-specific governance** and **frontier-model evaluation**. Our focus is the **practical adoption of open AI technologies across the whole stack** (open-weight models, agent harnesses and self-hosted infrastructure), for organisations **without large security or AI teams**.
