# Vision

> **Status: draft for discussion.** Comment in [Discussions](https://github.com/Dependable-AI-Initiative/dependable-ai-initiative/discussions) or open a pull request.

## The problem

Open AI technologies (open-weight models, open-source agent harnesses and self-hosted AI infrastructure) give organisations control, privacy and independence from single vendors. They are increasingly the practical choice for organisations that must keep data in-house or manage costs.

But adopting them safely is hard:

- **Responsibility shifts to the adopter.** With a hosted AI service, the provider carries much of the safety and security work. With open technologies, the organisation running them carries it.
- **Guidance is either too high-level or too specialised.** Governance frameworks describe *what* good looks like, but rarely *how* to check a specific model, agent setup or deployment. Technical security work often assumes a dedicated security team.
- **Evaluation results are scattered and hard to compare.** Organisations repeat the same assessments in isolation, and findings are rarely shared.
- **Many organisations lack in-house expertise.** Nonprofits, educational institutions, public bodies and small and medium-sized enterprises often adopt AI with little specialist support.

## Our vision

**Any organisation can adopt open AI technologies with confidence, knowing what to check, how to check it and what others have found.**

## Who we serve

- **Organisations adopting open AI**, especially those without large security or AI teams: nonprofits and NGOs, schools and universities, public bodies, and SMEs.
- **Practitioners**: IT leaders, architects, engineers and risk and compliance staff who need practical, actionable guidance.
- **The wider community**: researchers, vendors and standards bodies who benefit from shared, independent evaluation results.

## How we will get there

| Focus | What we deliver |
|---|---|
| **[Dependable AI Framework](../framework/)** | Practical guidelines for evaluating open-weight models, agent harnesses and AI infrastructure |
| **[Dependable AI Toolkit](../toolkit/)** | Open-source assessment tools, checklists and reference tests |
| **[Dependable AI Commons](../commons/)** | Community-maintained knowledge, evaluation results and implementation guidance |

## What we are not

- **Not a certification body** (at least initially). We provide methods and evidence; organisations make their own decisions.
- **Not a replacement for existing standards.** We build on and map to established work. See [related initiatives](related-initiatives.md).
- **Not a vendor.** See our [conflict-of-interest policy](../GOVERNANCE.md#conflicts-of-interest).
