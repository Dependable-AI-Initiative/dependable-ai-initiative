# Founding principles

> **Status: draft for discussion.** These principles guide what we build and how we decide. Proposed changes go through a [decision record](../decisions/).

1. **Practical first.** Every piece of guidance should help someone take a concrete action: a check to run, a question to ask, a setting to change.

2. **Proportionate.** Safety measures should fit the organisation's size, risk and resources. A small NGO and a bank need different depths of assessment; we provide both.

3. **Independent and vendor-neutral.** We do not favour any model, vendor or platform. Interests are declared, and people do not evaluate products they are connected to.

4. **Open by default.** Our work, discussions and decisions happen in public. Methods, tools and results are openly licensed so anyone can use, adapt and verify them.

5. **Evidence over opinion.** Claims are backed by tests, data or cited sources. Evaluations are reproducible: we publish versions, settings and methods.

6. **Build on existing work.** We map to and complement established standards and initiatives (e.g. NIST AI RMF, OWASP, ISO/IEC 42001, EU AI Act) rather than reinventing them.

7. **Community-maintained.** Knowledge stays current because practitioners keep it current. We make contributing easy and recognise contributors.

8. **Safety across the whole stack.** Dependability depends on the model, the agent harness around it and the infrastructure it runs on, not just the model alone.
