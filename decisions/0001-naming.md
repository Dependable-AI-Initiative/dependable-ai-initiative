# 0001. Naming of the initiative and its outputs

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-10-11
- **Proposed by:** @PatrickCSFan
- **Discussion:** _link to be added_

## Context

Early materials used two sets of names for the three focus areas:

| Descriptive name | Branded name |
|---|---|
| AI Adoption Safety Framework | Dependable AI Framework |
| AI Safety Evaluation Toolkit | Dependable AI Toolkit |
| AI Safety Commons | Dependable AI Commons |

Using both interchangeably risks confusing people into thinking these are six separate things.

## Decision

Use the **branded names** as the official names, with the descriptive phrase as a subtitle:

- **Dependable AI Initiative**: the nonprofit organisation
- **Dependable AI Framework**: its methodology (*practical guidelines for evaluating open-weight models, agent harnesses and AI infrastructure*)
- **Dependable AI Toolkit**: its open-source tools (*assessment tools, checklists and reference tests*)
- **Dependable AI Commons**: its community and shared resources (*knowledge, evaluation results and implementation guidance*)

## Options considered

1. **Branded names (proposed):** consistent identity, and every output carries the initiative's name. Less self-explanatory on its own, which the subtitles address.
2. **Descriptive names:** self-explanatory, but generic and harder to own, search for or associate with the initiative.

## Consequences

- All documents and the repository structure (`framework/`, `toolkit/`, `commons/`) use the branded names.
- The descriptive phrases appear as subtitles in READMEs and external materials.
